# 45. The desk manager's plan is structured, not prose

Status: Accepted. Extends ADR 41 (the plan) and ADR 43 (past days read back).

## Context

`set_manager_plan` took one text of up to 4,000 characters. On 16 Oct 2026 the manager wrote what the skill asked, "a few sentences", and the Manager page showed a 900-character paragraph: the price check, the desk's figures, the decision, its reasons, every overnight run with its stop and open money, a holding without a stop. Everything was there and the user couldn't read it. Half of it repeated what the page already shows (each line's capital and stop, the desk's money today), and no layout can group prose by decision.

## Decision

The plan takes three parts, each checked by `check_plan` in `core/agents/manager.py`:

- **`headline`:** the day's decision in one sentence, at most 300 characters. A longer one is refused with where the rest goes.
- **`items`:** one call per strategy, script or `own` (never the desk: its stop is a line): `runs` (enters or trades today), `holds` (keeps what's open, enters nothing new) or `sits_out` (doesn't trade today), each with a short `why`. A key named twice, an unknown strategy or script, or a blank why is refused. The strategy's or script's name is kept as it was when the plan was set.
- **`watch`:** at most 8 short sentences, what it keeps an eye on today.

The numbers stay out of the plan. Capital and stops are lines (`set_manager_numbers`), and the page shows each item beside its line and its money today, so the plan and the lines can't disagree in print.

**Storage.** The headline stays in `plan_text`; `plan_items` and `plan_watch` are new nullable JSON columns (ADR 18). `ManagerPlanSet` carries `items` and `watch`, so a past day reads them back from the audit log like the rest (ADR 43). A plan set before this has only its text, and the page shows it as before.

**The page.** The headline first; on today's plan, the desk's net today against its stop with the room left; then the items grouped Runs, Holds, Sits out, each with its line's stop and its money today; then Watching and the lessons. A past day shows the groups without today's figures: its numbers at the close are the table below.

## Consequences

- The skill's step 11 says what goes where. A model that still writes prose into the headline is refused at 300 characters, with the fix in the error.
- Old plans keep rendering as one paragraph; nothing migrates them.
