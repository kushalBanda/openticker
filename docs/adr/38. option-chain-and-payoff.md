# 38. Option chain page and payoff preview

Status: Accepted. Builds on ADR 16 (Black-76 on the forward), ADR 26 (baskets, buys first, and the broker's margin), ADR 28 (charges) and ADR 8 (tool design).

## Context

The web app's Option chain page lets a user put a position together from the chain and see what it makes or loses before placing it, without asking an agent. An agent should be able to ask the same question ("what does this iron fly pay at expiry?") and get the same answer. The chain as served had no bid or ask, so a position couldn't be priced where it would fill, and no futures, so a hedge with one couldn't be added.

## Decision

- **Payoff is pure maths in `core/options/payoff.py`.** At expiry an option is worth its intrinsic value and a future the underlying, so the curve is straight between strikes and every figure is exact: breakevens are the zero crossings, the most it makes and loses are at a strike or at zero, and a side is unbounded only when the curve still slopes past the highest strike (the underlying can't go below zero). Today's curve prices each option with Black-76 at its own implied volatility, solved from its last price with the underlying standing in for the forward, so today's curve passes through today's prices. Net delta is today's slope at the underlying, in rupees per point.
- **Options of one expiry only.** After the first expiry the rest of a calendar isn't a line any more; its payoff "at expiry" would need a model, and would be easy to misread. Futures may expire later: they are linear either way.
- **One read-only tool and its route**: `preview_payoff` (`POST /api/v1/options/payoff`), up to 20 legs on one underlying, each with a price to assume or the last price. The underlying and every leg are quoted in one call. 161 points across ±8% of the underlying, widened to take in every strike. A hosted script's key and a review's key reach it, as they reach the chain.
- **The chain carries each option's bid and ask** (an empty side, 0 from Kite, is `None`), its lot size, and the nearest two futures on the same underlying, quoted in the same call as the options.
- **The page polls the chain every 3 seconds.** Bid, ask, IV and Greeks come only from quotes; two quote calls a poll stay under Kite's one a second. The underlying's price itself is streamed.
- **Picking.** A bid sells, an ask buys, one lot at that price; the same way again adds a lot, the other way takes one off. The price is the user's to change before placing. The basket is kept per underlying, in the browser only.
- **Before placing**, the tray shows the net premium, the broker's margin for the whole set with its hedge benefit (`get_margin`, ADR 26; the broker's figure, not what the paper account blocks), and the charges each fill would pay at the sandbox's rates (ADR 28). It places as one basket of limit orders, buys first; a refused leg stays in the tray with the reason.
- **No change in open interest.** Kite's quote has no previous day's open interest, so the chain shows open interest only.

## Consequences

- The page and an agent get the same payoff for the same legs.
- A calendar or diagonal spread can be placed, but not previewed.
- The margin shown needs a broker session; without one the tray says why it has none.
