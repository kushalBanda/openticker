# 34. P&L history and the web app's Today

Status: Accepted. Builds on ADR 28 (every fill keeps its charges and what it realized).

## Context

The web app opens on the Dashboard, and the first question a user has there is "how am I doing today, after costs, and what happened while I was away?" Nothing recorded the answer. Positions carry only their unrealized P&L now; trades carry what each fill realized and paid (ADR 28), but nothing kept the account's P&L as it moved through a day, or each day's result, so a line through the session or a calendar of days could not be drawn, and an agent had no way to ask "how did last month go?". Recording must not guess: a position without a price is not worth zero.

## Decision

- **Today is worked out, never read back.** `use_cases/today.py` computes the day from its fills (realized P&L and charges, ADR 28) and the open positions as the broker marks them now. Open positions count by their change over the day: their unrealized P&L now less what they were worth at the last recorded close (`open_value`). Net after charges is realized plus that change less charges. An open position without a price makes the unrealized part and the net `None`, shown as "—", never 0.
- **Two tables, written by `openticker-serve`.** `PnlLoop` (daemon) records a point each minute of the NSE session into `intraday_pnl` (trading date, `HH:MM` exchange-local, net, realized, charges, unrealized), skipping a minute an open position can't be priced: a gap in the line, never a guess. After 15:35 it records the day into `daily_pnl` once, with `open_value` for the next day. When the broker can't price the positions then (the session expired), the day's last minute point marks them and the day is `estimated`, shown "(est.)". Minute points are kept 30 days; days are kept.
- **Only while the server runs.** Neither table is backfilled: a day or minute `openticker-serve` wasn't running for has no record, and the web app says so rather than drawing a flat line.
- **Two tools, MCP and REST.** `get_pnl_history(from_date, to_date)` returns recorded days, oldest first, with today worked out live while it isn't recorded yet (`live`). `get_charges_summary(from_date, to_date)` totals charges month by month and by kind, with what was charged before each kind was recorded (`unitemized`). At most 400 days per call.
- **"Since you were here."** A browser session keeps when its previous visit started: a request after 10 minutes idle starts a new visit (`core/web.py`). `GET /api/v1/today` (web app only) adds, when that previous visit was today, the fills since then, the stops, kills, expired sessions and refused orders (the three newest named, the rest counted) and how much the net moved since the last minute point before it. Identical events are said once, counted ("2 orders for NIFTY OCT FUT were refused").
- **First run is read, not stored.** `GET /api/v1/setup` answers from what exists: a stored broker session, instruments synced today, the first MCP client seen (ADR 35), the first paper fill. The Dashboard shows the checklist until a first fill.
- **The browser moves today with ticks.** The Dashboard's figure is the server's, moved by each open position's newer ticks (as Positions does); the minute line ends at that figure now, while the market is open. The tab title carries it, changed at most every 5 s.
- The reset (ADR 37) deletes both tables with the rest of the paper account.

## Consequences

- The Dashboard's Today, the daily calendar and the charges by month read these; so can an agent ("how did September go after costs?").
- Days before this record have no daily figure; their trades still show what they realized where it was recorded (ADR 28).
- A server stopped for part of a session leaves a gap in that day's line; the day itself is still recorded when the server next runs after the close on that day, not later.
