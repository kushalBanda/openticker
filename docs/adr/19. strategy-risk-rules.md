# 19. Strategy-wide risk rules

Status: Accepted

## Context

A multi-leg strategy (a straddle, an iron condor) is judged as a whole: the user sets a stop loss and target on the combined profit and loss, not only on each leg. Strategies that run unattended (ADR 12) need these rules evaluated on every price update, restored after a restart, and never fired on a guessed price. Per-position rules already exist as pure functions (ADR 9); the strategy rules sit on top of them.

## Decision

`core/risk/aggregate.py` holds `evaluate_strategy(risk, legs, prices)`, pure like ADR 9: no I/O, no clock, no logging. The caller persists the returned legs (each leg's trailed stop and best price), `peak_mtm`, `lock_floor` and `stops_at_entry`, and passes them back next time. Limits are `StrategyLimits`, in rupees, with losses given as positive amounts.

- **MTM** is every leg's realized P&L plus every open leg's unrealized P&L.
- **Order of evaluation:** each open leg's own rules (ADR 9), then stops to entry, then the whole-strategy rules in the order daily loss limit, profit lock, combined stop loss, combined target. The first one breached names the exit; any breach exits every open leg.
- **A missing price is never guessed.** If any open leg has no usable price, the whole-strategy rules are skipped and their state is carried unchanged; legs that do have prices are still judged on their own rules.
- **Profit lock** arms once the peak MTM reaches `arm_at` and exits everything when MTM falls to the floor. The floor starts at `lock` and never falls, not even if the lock is edited lower mid-run. In `lock_and_trail` the floor rises by `trail_step` for each `trail_step` the peak climbs beyond `arm_at`. Arming reads the persisted peak, so a lock earned before a restart still holds after it. The floor must be below `arm_at` (a floor at or above it would exit on the tick it arms), and a lock at zero is allowed. A floor left over from a lock that has since been removed is ignored.
- **Stops to entry**, when enabled, follow openalgo's rule: when one leg's stop loss fires, every other open leg's stop moves to its own entry price, and from then on the combined stop loss no longer applies (combined target, profit lock and daily loss limit still do). A leg is left alone when the move would loosen its stop, or when its entry is already on the wrong side of the market (the stop would close it at once, at a loss). Legs closing in the same evaluation are not moved. A target hit does not trigger it.
- **Daily loss limit** counts the strategy's earlier runs today (`earlier_runs_realized_pnl`) plus this run's MTM.

## Consequences

- Stops to entry can let a run end below zero with the combined stop loss off: the leg that stopped has already realized its loss, and the others can each fall back to entry. This matches openalgo and was chosen over a strategy-level breakeven floor.
- Each rule has a test that fails if it is removed or loosened (`tests/core/risk/test_aggregate.py`).
- Nothing calls `evaluate_strategy` yet; the strategy runner does.
