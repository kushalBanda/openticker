# 9. Per-position risk rules

Status: Accepted

## Context

A risk check that misfires either exits a position the user wanted to keep or fails to exit one they wanted protected. Risk engines in existing open-source trading platforms have shipped with specific, recurring defects:

- a position with a target but no stop loss gets an implicit stop at the entry price, and exits on the first move back through entry;
- a stop or target stored as `0` is read literally, so a short with stop `0` "breaches" on every tick (`price >= 0`);
- after a restart the trailing stop is re-armed off the current price instead of the best price seen, so a stop the position had earned silently falls back to its original level;
- a best price persisted as `0` stays the minimum forever on a short;
- the exit for a short is sent as another sell, doubling the position instead of covering it.

## Decision

`core/risk/position.py` holds the rules as pure functions over frozen value types (`core/risk/models.py`): no I/O, no clock, no logging. Callers load state, call `evaluate_position(risk, last_price)`, act on the decision, and persist the returned `stop_loss`, `highest_price` and `lowest_price`.

- **Absent means absent.** A price that is `None`, zero, negative or not finite is treated as missing. A missing stop loss is no stop loss; it never falls back to the entry price.
- **Direction lives in `side`, never in the sign of `quantity`.** Quantity must be positive. On a breach the decision carries `exit_side` (the opposite of `side`) and `exit_quantity`.
- **Evaluation order:** update the best price seen (from entry, or from this price if entry is missing), trail the stop, then test stop loss, target and capital cap, in that order. The stop is tested after trailing, so a stop that just moved can fire on the same tick.
- **Stop loss wins over target** when both are crossed in one evaluation: the order of the two touches is unknowable, so assume the adverse one.
- **Trailing** (`TrailingStop`) arms once the favourable move, measured from the **best price seen** (not the current price), reaches `trigger`. `CONTINUOUS` keeps the stop `step` behind the best price. `STEPPED` moves the stop `step` from the initial stop for every completed `trigger` of favourable movement. Either way the stop only tightens, and is never placed beyond the best price actually traded. A missing or invalid entry price disables trailing and P&L; absolute stop and target still apply.
- **Capital cap** breaches when `last_price * quantity` exceeds it.
- **An unusable last price** (zero, negative, not finite) returns `evaluated=False` with all state carried through unchanged.
- `validate_position` reports configurations that would exit immediately or trail badly (stop or target on the wrong side of the market, stepped trail with no initial stop or a step larger than its trigger), for callers to check before monitoring starts.

## Consequences

- Each defect above has a test that fails if it is reintroduced (`tests/core/risk/test_position.py`).
- Keeping only the favourable extreme (highest for longs, lowest for shorts) is enough for every rule here; adverse excursion is not tracked.
- Strategy-wide risk over several legs (combined stop loss, profit lock, stops to entry) builds on these rules in a separate module: ADR 19.
