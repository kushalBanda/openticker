# 14. Strategies are data, and survive restarts

Status: Accepted

## Context

A strategy runs for hours without anyone watching. A daemon restart in the middle must neither enter a position twice nor forget one it holds, and must keep the risk state it had built up (trailed stops, best prices, the locked-in profit floor).

## Decision

- **Strategies are definitions, not code:** an options kind (up to 10 legs chosen relative to the market: at the money or up to 5 strikes in or out, weekly/next-week/monthly/next-month expiry) and a signal kind (entries and exits from an alert URL, optionally long-only or short-only). Leg resolution, strategy-wide risk and signal mapping are pure functions in `core/strategies` and `core/risk/aggregate.py`.
- **Strategy-wide rules** on top of the per-position rules of ADR 9: combined stop loss and target on total P&L, a profit lock whose floor only rises (optionally trailing), moving every stop to breakeven (which then skips the combined stop loss but not the target), a daily loss limit across all of the day's runs, and square-off at a set time and on expiry day.
- **Every strategy order is written to `strategy_orders` before it is sent.**
- **Risk ratchets are checkpointed** whenever they move.
- **On start, the daemon reloads every run not yet ended.** Recorded orders decide what happened and what never happened; checkpoints decide the ratchets. An order recorded without a sandbox result is reconciled against the sandbox order book before the run resumes. A run that can't be reconciled stops with reason `recovery_failed` and notifies; it never guesses.
- Every run records its trigger, stop reason, realized P&L, peak and trough, and a timeline of events.

## Consequences

- An agent can create, preview, start and inspect a strategy through tools, then leave; a person reviewing later reads the run history and the audit log.
- The per-strategy and per-run P&L uses only that run's own tagged trades, never the account's net position.
