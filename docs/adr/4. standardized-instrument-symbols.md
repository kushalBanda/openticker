# 4. Broker-independent instrument symbols

Status: Accepted

## Context

Callers (agents, webhooks) name instruments by symbol. Broker symbols are inconsistent: Kite spells a weekly option `NIFTY2692223350CE` and a monthly one `NIFTY26SEP23350CE`, and each broker has its own. Quote and candle calls, meanwhile, need the broker's own identifiers.

## Decision

- Each `Instrument` carries both our `symbol` and the broker's `broker_symbol`/`token`, stored in the SQLite `instruments` table keyed by `(exchange, symbol)`, refreshed with `sync_instruments`.
- Standardized symbols:
  - equity and index: the broker's trading symbol (`RELIANCE`, `NIFTY 50`)
  - future: `<name><DDMMMYY>FUT` (`NIFTY29SEP26FUT`)
  - option: `<name><DDMMMYY><strike><CE|PE>` (`NIFTY22SEP2623350CE`); fractional strikes keep the decimal (`IDEA29SEP268.5PE`)
- Indices get their own `InstrumentType.INDEX`: they are not tradeable, but are needed as option underlyings.
- Supported exchanges: NSE, BSE, NFO, BFO, MCX. Rows for other exchanges are dropped at sync.
- Sync upserts; it does not replace the table.

## Consequences

- Checked against Kite's full instrument file: no `(exchange, symbol)` collisions.
- Expired contracts stay in the table until something prunes them.
- The table has no broker column; supporting a second broker needs one.
