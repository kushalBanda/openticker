# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Strategy runner (ADR 21): `start_strategy`, `stop_strategy`, `kill_strategy`, `release_kill_switch`, `close_strategy_leg`, `get_strategy_runs`, `get_strategy_run` and their `/api/v1` routes. `openticker-serve` enters a strategy's legs in the sandbox, then closes them on their own stops, targets and trailing stops and the whole run on the strategy-wide rules, at the intraday square-off, or on request. Every order is recorded before it is sent; ratchets are saved as they move. Starts, leg exits and stops are notified. A running strategy can't be edited or deleted. A leg left pending by an unexpected error or a daemon that stopped mid-entry is settled from the sandbox on the next step or stop, so a run never stays open forever.
- Strategy schedules (ADR 22): `schedule_strategy`, `unschedule_strategy` and `POST`/`DELETE /api/v1/strategies/{id}/schedule`. A scheduled strategy is entered at its entry time on its weekdays, skipping market holidays; an entry missed by more than a minute is skipped for the day. Every run closes at the strategy's exit time and, by default, on the expiry day of a contract it holds (stop reasons `schedule` and `expiry`). `get_strategy` shows `scheduled_broker`, `list_strategies` shows `scheduled`.
- Lost prices and recovery for strategy runs (ADR 23). A leg with no streamed price for `STRATEGY_TICK_FALLBACK_SECONDS` (default 10) is priced from quotes, polled every 2 seconds with a backoff on rate limits (Kite's HTTP 429 is now `BrokerRateLimitError`). A leg with no price from either source for `STRATEGY_TICK_STALE_SECONDS` (default 60), counted only while its market is open, stops the run with reason `tick_stale` and closes every leg; it doesn't resume on its own. Orders whose outcome a run never recorded (a crash or an error in between) are reconciled with the sandbox before the run carries on: fills are booked at their real price, orders never sent are sent again, and a mismatch stops the run with `recovery_failed`. On start, `openticker-serve` notes on each open run that it is watching it again. Runs record their lowest P&L (`trough_mtm`).
- Signal strategies (ADR 24): `create_signal_strategy`, `update_signal_strategy`, `rotate_strategy_webhook`, `disable_strategy_webhook`, `get_strategy_signals` and their `/api/v1` routes, plus the alert URL `POST /webhooks/strategies/{token}` on `openticker-serve`, which needs no API key. A signal strategy's legs name their contracts; TradingView-style JSON alerts (`long_entry`, `long_exit`, `short_entry`, `short_exit`) and ChartInk alerts (BUY, SELL, SHORT or COVER in the scan name) enter and exit them one at a time. An entry against a position held the other way closes it first; repeated alerts do nothing. The first entry of the day opens a run, which waits for more alerts until its exit time or the session's end. Alerts are checked for the kill switch, an optional IP allowlist, a rate limit of 100 a minute, the strategy's direction and entry window. Every alert is recorded without its token; the token is stored hashed, shown once, and kept out of the access log.
- Resting LIMIT, SL and SL-M sandbox orders (margin held while pending) filled by `openticker-serve` as live prices cross them; `cancel_order` tool and `DELETE /api/v1/orders/{order_id}`. Pending orders expire at the session's end. Intraday (MIS) positions are squared off 15 minutes before the close.
- Expiry settlement: positions in expired futures and options are closed at the underlying's closing price on expiry day (options at intrinsic value), including catch-up after the daemon was down. Notified as `PositionSettled`.
- Existing databases gain new columns automatically on upgrade (additive changes only, ADR 18).
- Market calendar with the 2026 NSE, BSE and MCX holidays, including MCX evening sessions and special sessions (`get_market_status` tool and route). Sandbox MARKET orders are refused while the exchange is closed.
- Live prices in `openticker-serve` from Kite's WebSocket ticker, for open sandbox positions and `OPENTICKER_WATCH`. Reconnects with backoff; an expired broker session sends one "log in" notification.
- REST API (`openticker-serve`) mirroring every MCP tool under `/api/v1`, with API keys (`openticker-serve keys create|list|revoke`) stored as hashes. Binds to `127.0.0.1:8750` by default (`OPENTICKER_BIND`).
- MCP server (`openticker-mcp`) with tools: `get_broker_login_url`, `connect_broker`, `sync_instruments`, `search_instruments`, `get_quote`, `get_historical_bars`.
- Zerodha (Kite Connect) broker adapter: login, instrument master, quotes, historical candles.
- Encrypted local storage of broker sessions (SQLite) and historical bars (DuckDB).
- Architecture decision records in `docs/adr/`.
- Event bus with an append-only audit log (`get_audit_log` tool) and notifications for orders and risk breaches via Slack Incoming Webhooks and SMTP email.
- Paper trading (`place_order`, `get_positions`, `get_funds`, `get_orderbook`): MARKET orders fill in a local sandbox at the broker's live price, with virtual capital, leverage-based margin, realized and unrealized P&L, an optional capital cap, and fill notifications. Nothing is sent to the broker.
- `evaluate_risk` tool: checks stop loss, target and capital cap settings against the live price.
- Option chains with implied volatility and Greeks (`get_option_chain`): Black-76 on the forward implied by the at-the-money pair, for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, NIFTYNXT50, SENSEX, BANKEX and stock options.
- Strategy definitions (`create_strategy`, `update_strategy`, `get_strategy`, `list_strategies`, `delete_strategy`, `preview_strategy`, and `/api/v1/strategies`, ADR 20): options strategies of 1 to 10 legs chosen relative to the market (ATM or N strikes in or out of the money, weekly, next week, monthly or next month expiry, or a future), with a schedule and strategy-wide limits. A preview resolves the legs to real contracts at the live price.
- Strategy-wide risk core (`core/risk/aggregate.py`, ADR 19): combined stop loss and target over a strategy's legs, profit lock and lock-and-trail, stops to entry when a leg's stop fires, and a daily loss limit.
- Risk core: per-position stop loss, target, continuous and stepped trailing stops, capital cap, and configuration validation (`core/risk`).
