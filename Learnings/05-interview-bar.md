# Elite quant interview bar

Verified 2026-08-30.

- **Mental math is a hard gate, not a soft skill.** Jane Street runs a mental-arithmetic test first — well-credentialed candidates fail it and get rejected with no appeal. Zetamac 60+ in 2 minutes correlates with passing; below 40 generally fails.
  - [Jane Street Interview Guide](https://www.techinterview.org/companies/jane-street/)
- **Probability under time pressure.** Optiver: 10 probability questions, 90 seconds each. Jane Street's later rounds: random walks, stopping times, multi-stage games, conditional probability, expected value under constraints.
  - [Inside the Jane Street, Citadel, Optiver, XTX Interviews](https://youngandcalculated.substack.com/p/inside-the-jane-street-citadel-optiver)
- **Market intuition + game theory**, especially Jump Trading (35% probability / 30% market intuition / 20% mental math / 15% behavioral). Real historical events come up: 2007 quant crisis, 2010 flash crash, 2020 oil futures collapse.
  - [Jump Trading Interview Process](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/jump-trading-interview)
- **Coding is a separate bar.** No computer in brainteaser rounds, but a standalone coding round expects real algorithms/data-structures competence, not scripting familiarity.

## Takeaway

Mental math and probability drills can't be crammed at the end — this is why the curriculum runs them in parallel from Phase 1, not only in Phase 5.
