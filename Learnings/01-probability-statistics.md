# Probability & statistics rigor

Why this matters: probability under time pressure is a core interview gate (e.g. Optiver: 10 questions, 90 seconds each). Descriptive stats (mean/variance from a pandas call) is not the bar — you need to reason from first principles, fast.

## Core topics

- Distributions (binomial, Poisson, normal, exponential) and when each applies
- Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem
- Expectation and variance — linearity of expectation as a fast-solve tool
- Random walks and stopping times (Jane Street favorite)
- Combinatorics fundamentals (fast counting under pressure)

## Resources

- *Fifty Challenging Problems in Probability* — Frederick Mosteller (also listed in Phase 5)
- Zetamac for daily mental math: https://zetamac.com
